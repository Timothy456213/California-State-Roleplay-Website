About us

California Roleplay is a community for players who enjoy realistic roleplay. Patrol as law enforcement, respond as fire and EMS, run a business, or live as a civilian. Everyone is welcome, and new players get help from our staff.

Server rules

  1. Respect every member and staff member.
  2. Stay in character during roleplay scenes.
  3. No random deathmatching or vehicle deathmatching.
  4. No cheating, exploiting, or using unauthorized mods.
  5. Follow staff instructions right away. Appeal decisions in a ticket.
  6. Keep chat free of hate speech and harassment.
