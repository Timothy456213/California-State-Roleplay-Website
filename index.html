About us

California Roleplay is a community for players who enjoy realistic roleplay. Patrol as law enforcement, respond as fire and EMS, keep the roads running, or live as a civilian. Everyone is welcome, and new players get help from our staff.

Departments

Law Enforcement. Patrol, traffic stops, investigations, and keeping the peace. Training is provided after you pass your application.
Fire and EMS. Respond to fires, crashes, and medical calls. Work with other departments to save lives in every scene.
Transportation. Clear roads, tow vehicles, and support scenes across the state.
Civilian. Build a character, get a job, buy a home, or start a business. No application needed to start.

Server rules

  1. Display NamesYou are required to have your Discord display name the same as your Roblox username. You will be automatically loaded in-game if you do not link your account.
  2. Respect OthersRemain respectful to both members and staff inside of our community. We will moderate you if we find you being disrespectful.
  3. Hate SpeechUsing racist, homophobic or genderphobia comments are prohibited, we ask that you keep these comments outside of our community.
  4. NSFW ContentSharing content made for adults will result in a permanent ban. We do not condone this inside of our community.
  5. Self PromotionDM advertising or advertising inside of our channels will result in a ban from our communications.
  6. Rule EnforcementStaff reserve the right to moderate you in the event you violate our servers regulations.
  7. LanguageOur community is English only. You are only permitted to speak English within our channels.
  8. Alt AccountsYou are allowed one account per-person inside of our server. If you are found to have multiple accounts inside of our server we will remove all accounts.
  9. Common SenseJust because a rule is not written down does not mean that we do not moderate it. You were given a brain for a reason, please use it.
  10. Voice ChatDo not abuse soundboards, voice calls or voice filters inside of our calls.
  11. Causing DramaWe ask that you keep all drama outside of our server. This keeps our community clean without having outside drama constantly being brought to our chats.
  12. Terms of ServiceYou are required to follow Discords Terms of Service at all times whilst on our community.
  13. Direct Message ModerationWe do not moderate members DMs unless it violates ToS or something that we do not side with. If these DMs are with a hired staff member, that is when we would initiate a further investigation.
